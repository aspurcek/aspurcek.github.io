Dragon Dogfight

Fire-breathing dragon air combat. Real-time 1v1 duels.

GET IT ON THE APP STORE

Free · iPhone & iPad · No ads · No in-app purchases · No account

Fly and fight

Steer with one thumb and hold FIRE with the other. Boost, dash, drop a smoke screen or launch a homing fireball, and watch your heat before the flame overheats.

Seven arenas

A castle on a lake, a skyscraper grid, neon Tokyo nights, a rainy dark city, a lava peak, the Mersin seafront and Devrim, a stadium in a pine forest on the Ankara steppe.

Duel your friends

Invite your Game Center friends to a real-time 1v1 duel, find a random opponent with Quick Match, or practise against bots any time, even offline.

Dragons and gear

Unlock four more dragon breeds as you level up, then collect skins, armour, saddles and horns with the scales you earn. Climb the Tower, the trophy board and the weekly event arena.
